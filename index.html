Trails

Routes we already know by weather.

Four walks from the river to the ridge. Distances are the path we actually take, including the stops.

  1. Cedar Switchback

    6.2 mi · moderate loop

    Old cedars, one creek crossing, and shade for most of the morning. Best after the leaves turn.

  2. Bluff Line

    9.4 mi · strenuous out-and-back

    A ridge above the river with wind and a long view. Pack a lunch and sit with your back to the rock.

  3. River Ford

    4.1 mi · easy loop

    Three rock crossings and an easy grade. A good first day for families who want to be back for a late lunch.

  4. Pine Bench

    3.3 mi · easy out-and-back

    An overlook for the last hour of light. In fall we carry headlamps for the walk down.
